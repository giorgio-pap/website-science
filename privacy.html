Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you open a page, your browser sends a request that typically includes your IP address, browser type, and the page you asked for. GitHub uses this to deliver the site. See GitHub’s own privacy statement for how they handle that.

Fonts

The typefaces Inter and Newsreader are stored on this site and served from the same host as the pages. Your browser does not contact Google to load them.

ListenBrainz

The home page can show the latest track from my ListenBrainz profile (MetaBrainz Foundation). Your browser requests that public feed from ListenBrainz. ListenBrainz can see your IP address. This is not advertising; it only fills the small listening card. If the request fails, the card stays hidden.

Stored on your device

The site stores two things in your browser’s local storage, on your device only:

  • your colour-theme choice
  • your analytics choice (accepted or declined)

These are not sent on to me.

Your rights

If you are in the EU, you can ask what data is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to processing. For this site the practical contact is the email above. You can also complain to the data-protection authority in your German state, or to the authority where you live.
